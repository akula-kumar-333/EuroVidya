## Brand & Style

This design system serves a specialized consultancy guiding Indian students and parents toward public and state-recognized higher education in Germany. The brand ethos rejects the hyper-promissory, transactional tropes of conventional educational agents. Instead, it positions itself as an unvarnished academic partner: analytical, legally precise, pragmatic, and transparent.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate/Modern structure** with **editorial calm and institutional rigor**. It draws inspiration from Nordic clarity and German administrative transparency: structured tables, unambiguous boundary declarations, high-contrast data presentation, and calm structural whitespace.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Pragmatic & Candid:** Replaces vague claims of "guaranteed admission" with APS audit steps, NC-cutoff realities, and Blocked Account requirements.
- **Dignified & Institutional:** Reassures conservative parent stakeholders via structured layouts, high-legibility typographic hierarchies, and restrained elevation.
- **Approachable & Precise:** Encourages students navigating complex visa regimes through deliberate callouts, step-wise timelines, and honest "What We Guarantee vs. What We Do Not" modules.

## Colors

The palette leverages academic authority through deep Prussian blue paired with action-oriented teal and institutional gold highlights. Surfaces remain airy and sterile to sustain focus across heavy documentation requirements.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#173B57`):** Command level. Reserved for primary structural headers, global navigation bars, key brand marks, and high-impact institutional framing.
- **Action Teal (`#167C80`):** Kinetic accent. Drives affirmative interactive states: primary buttons, active form field indicators, text links, step progressions, and interactive filters.
- **Warm Gold (`#E8B45B`):** Accreditation and status accent. Used strictly for eyebrow text, audit badges, visa checklist verifications, and non-alarmist attention markers. Never used for long-form body text.
- **Body Neutral (`#2D3741`):** Base typographic color. Provides sustained reading comfort without the jarring optical glare of pure black.
- **Muted Neutral (`#667784`):** Secondary metadata, table subtitles, inactive icon fills, and contextual captions.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F9FA`):** Cool off-white canvas minimizing eye strain across dense tabular data.
- **Tonal Tints:**
  - **Pale Blue (`#EAF2F5`):** Background fill for neutral context panels, requirement overviews, and parent briefing cards.
  - **Pale Teal (`#E6F2F1`):** Background fill for process milestones, success badges, and verified deliverables.
  - **Border Gray (`#D9E2E8`):** 1px structural framing defining table grids and card perimeters.

## Typography

Typography relies on `Inter` across all structural tiers to prioritize unambiguous cross-platform legibility, especially for non-native English speakers reading legal, academic, and financial stipulations.

### Rules of Usage
- **Strict Hierarchy:** Section titles must consistently employ `headline-lg` or `headline-md` paired with `label-eyebrow` in uppercase to set explicit context before copy starts.
- **Numeric Clarity:** Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enabled on all course fee tables, APS processing day counters, and German blocked account (€11,208/year) breakdowns.
- **Eyebrow Treatments:** `label-eyebrow` is styled with uppercase transform and colored using Warm Gold (`#E8B45B`) or Action Teal (`#167C80`) to categorize content without visual shouting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a standard 12-column fluid grid system anchored by strict content boundaries to prevent infinite horizontal stretching. Maximum content width is capped at `1200px` for landing patterns and `1360px` for comparison tables and portals.

### Breakpoints & Fluidity
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Sidebars for pathway navigation remain fixed while content streams freely.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Data comparison matrices collapse into tabbed cards or horizontal-scroll tables with clear visual cut-offs.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. All multi-column cards stack vertically; interactive tools (eligibility checks) transition to full-width steps.

Vertical cadence follows an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`), ensuring uniform whitespace across application milestone flows.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain academic credibility, this design system avoids heavy shadows, saturated glows, and dramatic 3D layers. Depth is established through **low-contrast outlines, subtle ambient diffusion, and planar tone-on-tone layering**.

### Spatial Layering Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pale Background (`#F7F9FA`). Base foundation for all full-bleed layouts.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border (`#D9E2E8`) and soft ambient grounding: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(23, 59, 87, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Active Surfaces):** Hover states on cards, sticky navigation bars, and active document preview panels: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(23, 59, 87, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Visa document uploaders, parent consultation booking dialogs: Pure White surface, `1px solid #D9E2E8`, supported by `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(23, 59, 87, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric envelope. Roundedness is disciplined: `0.25rem` (4px) base for form controls, tags, and table cells; `0.5rem` (8px) for containers and cards; `0.75rem` (12px) for high-level modals.

This structure communicates institutional precision and document-like sobriety, intentionally diverging from the toy-like, pill-heavy shapes prevalent in B2C tech apps. It preserves crisp corners that complement tabular records and official dossier layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Action Teal (`#167C80`) fill, White text, 4px border-radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding, weight 600. Focus ring: 2px offset in Action Teal.
- **Secondary Authority:** Transparent fill, 1.5px solid Primary Navy (`#173B57`), Primary Navy text. Hover brings a subtle background tint (`rgba(23, 59, 87, 0.04)`).
- **Tertiary / Text:** Action Teal text, underline on hover, inline baseline alignment with icon glyphs.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact labels with 4px border-radius, `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding, `0.75rem` font size.
  - *APS Verified:* Pale Teal (`#E6F2F1`) background with `#167C80` text.
  - *Public Uni / Tuition-Free:* Pale Blue (`#EAF2F5`) background with `#173B57` text.
  - *Realistic Assessment / Warning:* Pale Gold background (`#FDF8ED`) with `#B88728` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: White (`#FFFFFF`), Border: 1px solid `#D9E2E8`, Text: `#2D3741`.
- Active / Focus: Border changes to Action Teal (`#167C80`) with a 3px soft spread of `rgba(22, 124, 128, 0.15)`.
- Error: Border shifts to `#D64545`, accompanied by concise, unambiguous microcopy explaining the exact document deficiency.

### Explicit Boundary Cards ("What We Do vs. What We Do Not")
- Designed as a split dual-card or split table component.
- **Scope of Commitment (Left Side):** White background, top accent border of 3px Action Teal (`#167C80`). Green-tinted bullet indicators for verifiable deliverables (e.g., "Uni-Assist dossier verification", "VPD conversion auditing").
- **Reality Bounds (Right Side):** Pale Background (`#F7F9FA`), top accent border of 3px Muted Navy (`#667784`). Outlined cross indicators for non-promised items (e.g., "We do not sell fake work experience", "We do not guarantee public university admission without qualifying GPA").

### Data Tables (Course & Eligibility Matrices)
- Row height: 48px standard, alternating or bordered with 1px `#D9E2E8`.
- Header: Sticky, `#EAF2F5` background, uppercase `label-table` tracking, `#173B57` text color.
- Cell alignment: Alphanumeric left-aligned, monetary and ECTS numerical credits right-aligned with fixed tabular figures.

### Trust & Institutional Credibility Modules
- Minimalist seal or badge cards displaying German university affiliations (TU9, UAS7 recognition marks) anchored in monochrome slate, only gaining subtle natural color on interaction.
